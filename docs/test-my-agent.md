# Test my agent

See exactly what your agent would do before a real customer does. Nothing is sent to anyone.

**Where:** Settings, **AI controls**, **Test my agent**.

![Test my agent: type a customer text or pick something that happens in your app](https://speakvora.com/assets/guide/sheet-test-my-agent.jpg)

## Pretend to be a customer
1. Type what a customer would text, or tap an idea (**What are your hours?**, **I want to talk to a person**, and the words from your own workflows).
2. Press **Send**. You see the reply your agent would send, **why** (which workflow answered, or your AI), and anything else it would do: tell your team, text someone, add to a calendar.
3. If your AI is on, send a follow-up: it remembers the conversation, as it does for a real customer.

## Pretend something happened in your app
Pick **An order is ready**, **A reservation is made**, **A payment fails** and so on, then **Try it**. You see each text, email or team message the workflow would send, filled in with a sample customer and order.

## What the answers mean
| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| **Would hold the text… it is outside the allowed hours** | A safety rule (quiet hours, STOP, a limit, no consent) stops it. Real customers are protected the same way. |
| **It matched, but could not answer yet: Fill in the YOUR-... values** | Open Settings and fill in your business details (hours, address, review link). |
| **No workflow matched and your AI is off** | A real customer would get no automatic reply. Add a workflow for those words or switch your AI on. |
| **A real customer would be handed to your team** | Your staff phone or Slack would be contacted. |

## Safe by design
* Tests always run in practice mode, even if your agent is live. Nothing goes out, and nothing is saved as a real conversation.
* The customer is a made-up number. Typing STOP does not remove anyone from your real list.
* Your AI answers are real, so a test uses a small amount of your own AI key, like any answer.
