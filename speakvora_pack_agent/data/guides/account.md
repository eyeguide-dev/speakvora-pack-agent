# Connect your Speakvora account

Pairing this agent with your Speakvora account lets it create your hosted order pages, voice new lines, and ask for missing lines. Only order numbers and steps are sent for order pages, never customer details.

## Steps
1. Sign in at speakvora.com and open **Account**.
2. Open the **Voice API** tab and press **Create key**. Give it a name such as "My agent" and copy the key (shown once).
3. In the agent open **Connect, Your Speakvora agent**, paste the key under Speakvora API key, and press **Save and test**.
4. If you use order tracking links, press **Create my order pages** in the Orders tab.

## Good to know
- Keep the key private. If it leaks, delete it on your Account page and create another.
- The agent works offline for everything already in your bundle; the key is only needed for new lines and hosted pages.
